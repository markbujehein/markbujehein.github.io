I am a nanoscientist working between experiment and computation. I completed my MSc in Nanoscience at the
University of Copenhagen in June 2026 (thesis graded 12, GPA 9.3) and am currently a guest researcher at the
Niels Bohr Institute, where I am drafting a first-author manuscript from my thesis with
Assoc. Prof. Heloisa N. Bordallo and collaborators at the ISIS Neutron and Muon Source.

My research centres on **neutron and X-ray scattering**, especially data analysis where low signal-to-noise
is the main challenge. I connect computational models (Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics, DFT) to measured data,
with an emphasis on uncertainty quantification. I have taken part in six beamtimes at ISIS, MAX IV, PSI and
Diamond Light Source, including a four-day neutron spin echo experiment on CAMEA at PSI.
My MSc thesis determined the neutron total cross section of thymol and p-cymene from neutron transmission.

I also write scientific software and use machine learning where it fits: I developed a graph neural network for
test beam data from the ALICE@CERN FoCal-E detector upgrade, and in October 2026 I join the Data Analysis and
Modelling group at the European Spallation Source DMSC to implement new models in the open-source EasyScience
libraries.

I am looking for a PhD position in scattering-based materials research. See my [projects]({filename}/pages/projects.md)
and [CV]({filename}/pages/cv.md) for more.
