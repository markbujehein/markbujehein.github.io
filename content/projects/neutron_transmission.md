My MSc thesis at the University of Copenhagen is titled
*"Neutron Total Cross Section for Thymol and p-Cymene Obtained From Neutron Transmission"*.
It deals with neutron moderator materials and combines neutron transmission measurements with
deep-inelastic neutron scattering (DINS).

The thesis was supervised by Assoc. Prof. Heloisa N. Bordallo (Niels Bohr Institute) and
Dr. Svemir Rudić (ISIS Neutron and Muon Source). A first-author manuscript based on it is currently in preparation.
