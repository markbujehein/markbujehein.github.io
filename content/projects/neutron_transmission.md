My MSc thesis at the University of Copenhagen, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Heloisa N. Bordallo (Niels Bohr Institute)
and Dr. Svemir Rudić (ISIS Neutron and Muon Source), is titled
*"Neutron Total Cross Section for Thymol and p-Cymene Obtained From Neutron Transmission"*.

The work obtains the total neutron cross section of the two molecules from neutron transmission measurements.
A first-author manuscript based on the thesis is currently in preparation.
