In the University of Copenhagen course *Quantum Phenomena in Nano Systems* I fabricated a semiconductor Hall bar in the
cleanroom as a course exercise and measured it at low temperature, about 4 K and about 0.4 K, to see how strongly temperature
affects whether quantum effects can be observed.

The theory behind these experiments was covered further in the MSc course *Quantum Nanoelectronics*:
electron transport in nanostructures, the Landauer formalism, Hall effects, quantum dots and spin qubits.
