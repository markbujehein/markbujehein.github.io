During my MSc I carried out six beamtimes at four large-scale facilities:

- **ISIS Neutron and Muon Source:** TOSCA (inelastic neutron scattering) and POLARIS (neutron powder diffraction), including gas loading into a cryostat reaching about 12 K.
- **MAX IV:** CoSAXS (small-angle X-ray scattering).
- **Paul Scherrer Institute:** CAMEA (cold-neutron multiplexing). A four-day neutron spin echo experiment used multiplexing of the polychromatic incoming beam to capture more information in shorter time, easing the low counting statistics of neutron scattering.
- **Diamond Light Source:** VerSoX (soft X-ray spectroscopy).

My tasks included sample preparation, gas loading and in-situ data analysis. I was trusted to support experiments
independently for my thesis supervisor and departmental researchers, and I wrote beamtime proposals and
prepared scientific posters and presentations.
