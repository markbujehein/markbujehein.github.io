During my MSc I took part in six beamtimes at large-scale facilities: ISIS, MAX IV, PSI and Diamond Light Source.

- Four-day neutron spin echo experiment on CAMEA at PSI, using beam multiplexing to raise the data acquisition rate.
- Sample preparation, gas loading and in-situ data analysis.
- Beamtime proposals, scientific posters and academic presentations.
