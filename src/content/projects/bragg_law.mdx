An interactive [marimo](https://marimo.io) notebook. It runs entirely in your browser (Python via WebAssembly), so there is
no server behind it. A crystal with lattice-plane spacing $$d$$ diffracts radiation of wavelength $$\lambda$$ where
$$n\lambda = 2d\sin\theta$$, which gives the scattering angle

$$
2\theta = 2\arcsin\left(\frac{n\lambda}{2d}\right).
$$
