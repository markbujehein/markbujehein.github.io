I am a nanoscientist working between experiment and computation. I completed my MSc in Nanoscience at the
University of Copenhagen in June 2026 (thesis graded 12) and am currently a guest researcher at the
Niels Bohr Institute, where I am drafting a first-author manuscript from my thesis with
Assoc. Prof. Heloisa N. Bordallo and collaborators at the ISIS Neutron and Muon Source.

My research centres on **neutron and X-ray scattering**, especially data analysis where low signal-to-noise
is the main challenge. I connect computational models (Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics, DFT) to measured data,
with an emphasis on uncertainty quantification. I have taken part in six beamtimes at ISIS, MAX IV, PSI and
Diamond Light Source, including a four-day experiment on CAMEA at PSI measuring magnon dispersion in
antiferromagnetic MnWO4. In my MSc thesis I derived energy-dependent total neutron cross sections from
VESUVIO transmission data and compared them with cross sections calculated in NCrystal.

I also write scientific software and use machine learning where it fits: in an Applied Machine Learning
group exam project I developed a graph neural network for test beam data from the ALICE@CERN FoCal
detector upgrade, and in October 2026 I spent four weeks in the Data Analysis & Modelling group at the
European Spallation Source DMSC implementing new models in open-source neutron data analysis libraries (EasyScience).
