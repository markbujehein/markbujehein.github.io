---
term: SAXS
name: Small-angle X-ray scattering
projects: [beamtimes]
---

X-rays scattered at small angles probe structures of roughly 1 to 100 nanometres. At small angles the scattering depends on the shape and size of nanometre-scale structures such as particles, pores and polymers, not on atomic positions. CoSAXS at MAX IV is a small-angle scattering beamline.
