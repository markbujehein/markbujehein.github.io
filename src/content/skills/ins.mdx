---
term: INS
name: Inelastic neutron scattering
summary: Neutrons exchange energy with a sample, which reveals its vibrations and other excitations.
projects: [beamtimes]
---

In inelastic neutron scattering the neutron gains or loses energy in the sample. Measuring that energy change as a function of momentum transfer shows how the atoms vibrate and move. The TOSCA instrument at ISIS is built for this.
