---
term: INS
name: Inelastic neutron scattering
projects: [beamtimes]
---

A vibrational spectroscopy technique that studies the motion of atomic nuclei directly, so no model of the electrons' response is needed. It is usually applied to solids at low temperature. Unlike optical methods it has no symmetry selection rules, and its signal is dominated by scattering from hydrogen atoms [@sciencedirect-ins].
